About

Capital, policy and technology, from the same seat.

Michael Benezra is Partner, North America at Crewstone International, a globally active private equity firm, where he focuses on investments across consumer, financial services, life sciences and commercial real estate.

He is also CEO and co-founder of Signal87 AI, which he co-founded with Michael Chavira. He leads product design and strategy for citation-backed document research systems serving legal, legislative, municipal and enterprise teams. The platform helps users research and compare documents, with answers linked to their sources.

His work spans legal AI strategy, enterprise document memory, AI governance and municipal technology. He also serves on the advisory board of the Alliance for American Leadership.

Before moving into investing, he served as Israel's Consul for Innovation & Economic Affairs in New England, working with governors, state economic development teams and federal officials on trade and investment. He went on to co-found the GK Fund, a Boston nonprofit closing the racial wealth gap in startup funding. As chief operating officer of Colette Phillips Communications, he helped lead All Inclusive Boston, the City of Boston's tourism recovery campaign showcasing the city's diverse neighborhoods. He holds a master's degree in government from Harvard University.

  3. 2020 – 2022

    Co-founder & Executive Director, The GK Fund

    Co-founded the GK Fund in December 2019 with Colette Phillips and Andre Porter. A Boston-based 501(c)(3) racial equity nonprofit, the GK Fund works to close the racial wealth gap by giving $10,000 unrestricted micro-grants — no equity, no notes — together with mentorship, marketing support and pro bono legal services through a partnership with Hinckley Allen, to BIPOC-, women-, LGBTQ- and veteran-founded companies across Greater Boston and Massachusetts Gateway Cities. The fund awarded its inaugural $100,000 in micro-grants to six companies in November 2021, raised $600,000 in its first year, and was backed by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts with a $100,000 budget earmark. Supporters include PNC Bank, the Barr Foundation and The Boston Foundation. He tells the fund's story on the Giant Robots podcast.

  4. 2020 – 2022

    Chief Operating Officer, Colette Phillips Communications

    Helped lead All Inclusive Boston, the City of Boston's tourism recovery campaign, developed by Colette Phillips Communications with Proverb and the Greater Boston Convention & Visitors Bureau and launched in 2021 under Mayor Kim Janey to showcase Boston's diverse neighborhoods. The campaign grew out of a City of Boston tourism recovery RFP the team won in 2020 — at the time, the largest contract the city had awarded to a minority-owned company. He discusses the campaign on the Giant Robots podcast.

  5. 2017 – 2020

    Consul for Innovation & Economic Affairs, Consulate General of Israel to New England

    Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Led economic diplomacy and bilateral trade work across New England, helping Israeli companies secure partnerships and capital. Worked on energy modernization in Cabo Verde through the Power Africa initiative and on U.S.–Israeli industrial collaboration with the BIRD Foundation.
