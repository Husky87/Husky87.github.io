About this record

This page collects the documented public record of Michael Benezra’s work in government, law, and technology. It spans his service as a Legislative Director in Massachusetts state government, as Consul for Innovation & Economic Affairs for Israel in New England, as staff to a Massachusetts legislative task force, and in nonprofit board service — each entry linked to its source. For the narrative version, see his biography.
