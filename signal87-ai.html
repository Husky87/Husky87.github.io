Signal87 Ai

Michael Benezra is the Founder & CEO of Signal87 Ai, the document-intelligence platform he co-founded with Michael Chavira in 2024. Signal87 Ai serves legal, compliance, real estate, and government teams — the teams whose work lives in documents: contracts, filings, records, and reports.

The problem Signal87 Ai addresses is simple to state. Search finds files, not answers. General chatbots have never seen your documents, and cannot show where an answer came from. The heavy platforms that can work across a full collection take months and large budgets to put in place. So the people who most need reliable answers from their own records are left opening files one at a time.

Signal87 Ai closes that gap. Users ask a question in plain language across an entire collection at once — a contract file, a set of meeting minutes, a budget archive — and every answer is traced back to the exact source it came from, so results can be checked, cited, and relied on in work where accuracy is not optional.

In November 2026, Signal87 Ai was accepted into the OpenAI Partner Network — see the press release and its publication on openPR.
