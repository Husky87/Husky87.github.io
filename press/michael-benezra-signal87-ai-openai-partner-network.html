Signal87 Ai, Led by Founder Michael Benezra, Accepted into the OpenAI Partner Network

BOSTON, Mass. — November 6, 2026 — Signal87 Ai, the document-intelligence platform founded by Michael Benezra, today announced that it has been accepted into the OpenAI Partner Network.

Signal87 Ai gives legal, compliance, real estate, and government teams a way to get answers out of their own documents. Instead of searching file by file, users ask a question in plain language across an entire collection at once, and every answer is traced back to the exact source it came from — so results can be checked, cited, and relied on in work where accuracy is not optional.

“Getting information out of documents is still harder than it should be,” said Michael Benezra, Founder and CEO of Signal87 Ai. “Search finds files, not answers, and general chatbots can’t work from your documents or show you where an answer came from. We built Signal87 Ai to close that gap, and joining the OpenAI Partner Network is a meaningful step in bringing citation-backed document intelligence to more of the teams who need it.”

Signal87 Ai is used by teams whose work lives in documents — contracts, filings, records, and reports — including in government, where the platform is being tested on the document collections public servants work with every day.
