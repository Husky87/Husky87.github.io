Essays on AI, documents, and public records · Boston

Essays

Michael Benezra writes about the documents public institutions run on — the meeting minutes, contract files, budget books, and permit records where the actual decisions live — and the AI that can finally read them at scale, with every answer traced to its source. The essays are published on Substack and mirrored on Hashnode. They are the thinking behind Signal87 Ai, the document-intelligence platform he founded.
