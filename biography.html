Biography

Michael Benezra is the Founder & CEO of Signal87 Ai, a document-intelligence platform built for legal, compliance, real estate, and government teams whose work lives in documents. Signal87 Ai lets those teams ask every document at once, in plain language, and get answers traced to the source. He is based in Boston, Massachusetts, and was named to the Boston Business Journal’s 40 Under 40 in 2021.

Michael Benezra earned a Harvard A.M. in American Government in 2015 and a B.A. from the University of Washington in 2011. At Harvard Law School, he served as a Research Director at the Harvard Law School Project on Disability. Earlier, in Massachusetts state government’s disability and higher-education work, he served as Task Force Staff for the Massachusetts Task Force on Higher Education for Students with Intellectual Disability and Autism, whose report to the Massachusetts Legislature was delivered in April 2014.

His public-service career began in Washington, where he was a Legislative Aide in the U.S. House of Representatives in 2009. He returned to Massachusetts as Legislative Director of the Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities from 2015 to 2017. In that role he was also the Department’s named contact for the Rural Policy Advisory Commission’s working groups in 2017, fielding requests for the commission’s Infrastructure Working Group, its Workforce/Demographics Sub-Committee, and the full commission.

From 2017 to 2020, Michael Benezra served as Consul for Innovation & Economic Affairs at the Consulate General of Israel in New England, promoting technology and economic ties between Israel and the region. The Boston Business Journal covered Massachusetts technology firms expanding to Israel during his tenure, and the Rhode Island House of Representatives welcomed him with the Consulate delegation to its chamber for Israel Independence Day in 2018 and 2019.

In civic life, Michael Benezra co-founded GK Fund Inc, a Boston 501(c)(3), and served as its Board Co-Chair from 2020 to 2024. He serves on the Community Board of Advisors of the Massachusetts Association for Mental Health, which listed him in its 2022 and 2023 annual reports. Across these roles — government, diplomacy, higher education, and civic leadership — his work has centered on helping institutions understand and act on their own information.
